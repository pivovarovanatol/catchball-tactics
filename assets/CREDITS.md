# Asset credits

## player.glb

Both by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain). Thank you, Quaternius.

- **Character:** the female body (Superhero_Female) and hairstyles (Long, Buns, Simple Parted, eyebrows) from
  **Universal Base Characters** (Standard).
- **Animations:** Idle, Crouch Idle, Jog, Sprint and Jump Loop from the **Universal Animation Library** (Standard),
  retargeted onto the character's skeleton (rotations, plus the hip track scaled to her height).

Trimmed for this project: one skeleton shared by body and hair, extra UV/colour channels and normal/roughness maps
removed, textures resized to JPEG, keyframes resampled. The kit, socks and sneakers, the waiting stance, throws,
passes, blocks and catches are made in code (`js/view3d.js`) on top of these clips.
